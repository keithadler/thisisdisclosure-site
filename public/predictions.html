Evidence I am pointing to

For the liquid or mist speculation. This is a pointer, not proof.

In November 2014 a Chilean Navy helicopter’s infrared camera recorded an object that appeared to release two plumes of gas or vapor, visible only in infrared. In 2017 Chile’s CEFAA classified it as an unidentified aerial phenomenon. It said the object could not have been a military or civilian aircraft and that the plumes were not waste water. This was Chile’s investigation, not the U.S. AARO’s, and I found no AARO statement on it.
The War Zone report · Documents released on the case
