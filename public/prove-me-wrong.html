The test, in my words
If a higher intelligence is not acknowledged by a major world power by the end of 2028, I’m wrong.

To keep myself honest, here is the plain reading I will hold myself to: an official, on-the-record statement by the government of a major world power. Not a leak, not an anonymous source, not a rumor. The date is written down on the predictions page, with a fingerprint of the exact text, so it cannot be changed later.
