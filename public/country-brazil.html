Country guide

Brazil.

Brazil's Penal Code makes failing to help someone in need an offense (article 135, omissao de socorro), and the Constitution lists building a solidary society among its fundamental objectives. Federal laws regulate volunteer service (Law 9.608/1998) and partnerships between public bodies and nonprofit civil society organizations (Law 13.019/2014).

Is there a legal duty to help a stranger?

Yes

Article 135 of Decreto-Lei 2.848/1940 punishes failing to help, when it can be done without personal risk, an abandoned or lost child or an injured, helpless person in serious and imminent danger, or failing to call public authorities in those cases. The penalty is detention of one to six months or a fine, increased by half if serious bodily injury results and tripled if death results. Article 135-A separately punishes demanding a guarantee as a condition for emergency medical care. Translation from the Portuguese statute is this guide's own.

Decreto-Lei No. 2.848, de 7 de dezembro de 1940 (Codigo Penal), art. 135 (Presidencia da Republica (planalto.gov.br))

Are people who help protected?

No general duty found

No Brazilian statute was found in the sources reviewed that specifically protects good-faith helpers from liability. Law 13.722/2018, which requires first-aid training in schools, is a training mandate and not a liability shield.

How the government promotes giving and service

Constitution, article 3Among the fundamental objectives of the Republic the Constitution lists building a free, just and solidary society (sociedade livre, justa e solidaria).Presidencia da Republica (planalto.gov.br)Lei 9.608/1998 (voluntary service)The law defines voluntary service as unpaid activity by an individual for a public body or nonprofit institution with civic, cultural, educational, scientific, recreational or assistance purposes. It states that volunteer service creates no employment relationship and allows reimbursement of documented expenses.Presidencia da Republica (planalto.gov.br)Lei 13.019/2014 (Marco Regulatorio das Organizacoes da Sociedade Civil)The law sets general rules for partnerships between public administration and civil society organizations, and creates the termo de colaboracao and termo de fomento instruments. Its article 5 lists social participation and strengthening civil society among its foundations.Presidencia da Republica (planalto.gov.br)Lei 13.722/2018 (first aid training)The law requires public and private basic education schools and child recreation establishments to train teachers and staff in basic first aid, with courses offered annually.Presidencia da Republica (planalto.gov.br)

One thing worth knowing

Brazil's duty-to-assist offense sits in the Penal Code alongside a constitutional objective of a solidary society.
