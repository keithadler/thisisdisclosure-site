Sources

Every quotation on this site is a short excerpt from a work in the public domain in the United States (or a United States government work, or a United Nations document). Each is shown with who said or wrote it, in what work, and when, and with the translator where there is one. The wording was checked against the source linked below.

No endorsement. Every person, author, scholar, institution, government body, religion and tradition named or quoted here appears only for attribution or reference. None of them has reviewed, approved, sponsored or endorsed this site or my ideas, and none is affiliated with it. The one exception is me: I am Keith Adler, I wrote this site, and I endorse it. A quotation shows what someone wrote or said, not that they agree with me. The studies, documents and events cited are shown as public facts and records; the interpretations and conclusions drawn from them are mine alone.

If you think anything is misattributed, or you hold rights in something shown here, please tell me through the address at the end of the site, and I will look at it.
