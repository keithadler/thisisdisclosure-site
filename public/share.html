Words to send

Use them as they are, or say it your way.

One line

Treat each other like humans. thisisdisclosure.com

Short

A message for everyone, 18 and over: how we treat each other may be what counts most, especially when nobody is watching and nothing comes back. One person's hypothesis, not a demand. thisisdisclosure.com

Longer

I came across a site with one question on it: Is this what a human being does? It argues that how we treat each other, especially strangers and especially unseen, may be what is being measured. It is a hypothesis, it asks for nothing, and it has plain cheat sheets for elders, teens, parents and everyone else. Take a look when you have ten minutes: https://thisisdisclosure.com

Start a ten-minute conversation

For a kitchen table, a classroom, a staff meeting or a congregation. You do not need to believe the hypothesis to have this conversation.

  1. Ask the group the one question: Is this what a human being does?
  2. Ask for one real example from the last week, big or small, of someone doing good with nothing expected back.
  3. Read one of the cheat sheets that fits the room, and let each person pick one thing to do this week.
  4. Next time, ask how it went. Do not rank anyone or keep score.
