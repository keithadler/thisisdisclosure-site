Guides by tradition.

Many traditions already hold ideas close to this one: that what you do for strangers, unseen and for nothing, matters most. Find yours and see it in your own texts, in your own words.

These guides do not say a religion agrees with me. They point to the tradition's own public-domain texts and teaching so you can judge for yourself. If your tradition is missing, tell me at keith at thisisdisclosure dot com and I will research it with the same rules.
