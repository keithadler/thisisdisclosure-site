Caveat: the size of this effect is debated. A 1994 meta-analysis by Judy Cameron and W. David Pierce, “Reinforcement, reward, and intrinsic motivation,” Review of Educational Research 64(3), 363–423, found the effect limited to certain kinds of reward and conditions.
